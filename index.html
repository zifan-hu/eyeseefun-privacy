This Privacy Policy explains how EYE SEE FUN (the “Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you install and play the Game on an Android device. By installing or using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

The Game is developed and published by an independent developer (“the Developer”). For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at yugreatgame@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The Game is designed to be played entirely on your device and does not require an account, a sign-in, or an internet connection to be played.

Specifically, the Game:

  • does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number;
  • does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or calendar;
  • does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking technologies;
  • does not transmit gameplay data, device identifiers, or usage statistics off your device.

Game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are not backed up to, or readable by, any server operated by us.

3. Information collected by Google Play

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google may automatically collect certain information in connection with your download, installation, and use of the Play Store and Google Play services — for example installation events, crash and performance diagnostics (“Android vitals”), and payment information for any purchase you make through Google Play. That collection is carried out by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy, not by us. We may see aggregated, non-identifying statistics in the Google Play Console (such as country-level install counts), and we do not use them to identify individuals.

4. How we use information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use it for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any other purpose. Local game data is used only to let the Game work as intended — for example to remember your settings and progress.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade personal information, because we do not collect it. We have no third-party advertising, analytics, or data-broker partners in the Game.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at yugreatgame@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and security

We retain no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Data stored locally by the Game remains on your device until you clear the Game’s data or uninstall it. Uninstalling the Game removes that local data.

8. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold any personal data about you, we cannot disclose, correct, or delete records we do not have. You can still contact us at yugreatgame@gmail.com with any request and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

9. International users

The Game does not transfer personal data across borders, because it does not collect any. Any data handled by Google Play is processed by Google under its own terms and safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game’s features change — for example if a future version adds online features, advertising, or analytics. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the Game or on Google Play. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
